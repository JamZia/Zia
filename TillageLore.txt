     They call it the Dark Cover.
At first, I thought it was just the undead — night-walkers risen from graves, dragging themselves across the fields. I figured if I kept my head down, built a fence, maybe planted a few crops, I’d be safe.

But I’ve seen too much now.
And I’ve heard enough to know: the Dark Cover isn’t just the dead. It’s what brings them.

The locals don’t speak of it loudly, but when they do... they use one word: Biomancy.
Dark, twisted magic. Old, forbidden. It's not cast from towers or scrolls — it's carved into flesh. Grown. Changed.

There are people out there, hiding in the deep — warping animals into monsters, stitching life into something wrong. Creatures that bleed green and shriek like nothing natural. And sometimes... they wear faces that used to belong to something else.

They say the Cover isn’t spreading by chance. It’s being built. Cultivated. Fed.

And I’m standing on the edge of it.

I came here with nothing but farming hands, hoping to claim a patch of land and live quiet.
But now I know the truth: if I want to hold this land, I need to be more than a farmer.

I need to prepare. To build, to grow strong, to understand what I’m up against.
Because the Dark Cover is not just rising.

It’s awakening.
