# ☀ Welcome to the Tillage 3.0 Beta.
Tillage III is the most polished version yet.
The update adds full singleplayer support, seamless co-op, a new reputation system for multiplayer, and a tutorial world for new players.
Performance is better, the UI is improved, and tons of features—from dynamic structures to roleplay systems—have been added or reworked.
The mod list was carefully refined, cutting weaker mods and replacing them with ones that add real depth. Enjoy!
