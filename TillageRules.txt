## Rules & Guidelines
• No cheating.
This includes using unauthorized mods, x-ray packs, glitch abuse, or any form of automation that bypasses intended gameplay. If you’re unsure whether something is allowed, ask staff first.

• Player VS Player.
PvP must always be mutual. You can only engage in combat if both parties agree. Traps are allowed only if you're already in active conflict with that player. Trapping peaceful or uninvolved Tillagers is not allowed.

• Caution.
Be cautious about moving in with or joining bases with unfamiliar players. Conflicts over items, space, or playstyles can be avoided by building trust before collaborating closely.

• Roleplay.
Let people play how they want, within the rules. Don’t try to control, interrupt, or override another player’s in-game decisions, paths, or experiences without talking to them first.

• Theme.
Tillage is a medieval survival roleplay server. Modern, futuristic, or heavily out-of-place structures break immersion and may be removed. Staff are happy to give feedback if you're unsure.

• Resources.
Don’t hand out items, invite others into your build, or team up without agreement from everyone involved. Even generosity needs consent—check in first to avoid misunderstandings.

• Enjoy Tillage.
Take your time, explore and build your place in the world. We’re here for story, survival, and shared experiences—help make it memorable for everyone.
