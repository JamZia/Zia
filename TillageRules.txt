## Rules & Guidelines
• No cheating.
Glitches, x-ray, unfair automation, and outside mods are not allowed. Only use mods in the official Tillage modpack. Ask staff if you're unsure.

• No Stealing.
Thievery is not allowed unless you are in an active, declared feud with the Tillager. Random or sneaky theft without real reason breaks the spirit of fair play and will be treated as rulebreaking.

• Player VS Player.
PvP must always be mutual. You can only engage in combat if both parties agree. Retaliation via PvP must be appropriate. Traps are allowed only if you're already in active conflict with that Tillager.

• Caution.
Be cautious about moving in with or joining bases with unfamiliar Tillagers. Conflicts over items, space, or playstyles can be avoided by building trust before collaborating closely.

• Roleplay.
You must have consent before significantly altering another Tillager’s story. This includes killing important characters, destroying builds, or forcing unwanted narratives. If unsure, don't do it!

• Theme.
If a build breaks the medieval theme, staff may ask for changes or remove it. This includes overly modern, sci-fi, or meme-like designs. Use medieval fantasy inspiration. If unsure, ask.

• Resources.
Don’t hand out valuable items or access unless you trust the Tillager. Staff won’t refund losses due to miscommunication, unless rules were clearly broken.

• Enjoy Tillage.
Take your time, explore and build your place in the world. We’re here for story, survival, and shared experiences—help make it memorable for everyone.

These rules aren’t exhaustive. If something violates the spirit of fair play or harms the community experience, staff may act even if it's not explicitly listed here.
