## Server Overview
• Annual World Reset — The map resets every year (around July) to keep things fresh and balanced.

• Player-Driven Roleplay — The server embraces RP as a core part of its experience. Build stories, kingdoms, alliances, and rivalries.

• Staff Support — Our staff team is dedicated to ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for all.
Need help? Reach out anytime.

• Strikes and Reports:
Tillage MP uses a three-strike system. Breaking any rule will result in a strike. False or malicious reports are strictly prohibited. Abusing the report system by making up reports, spamming staff or using it to harass others will result in a strike.
