# 3.0.4 Changelog: (7/18/25)

## Changes:
- Changed Mutliplayer Rules.
Added extra clarification and safety precautions.
- Added Crawling (X by default)
- Added Biomantic Delight.
Adds new items and recipes for meals from Biomancy.
- Added Dew Drop & compats.
Adds watering cans and gameplay mechanics. (Called "Spoutpots" in-game)
- Updated Lit it Up.
Furnace UI now has a button with info. Switchgrass is now a stage crop.
- Removed Unlit Campfire mod.
New mechanics in Lit it Up make this useless.
- Nerfed Player health gain. (Multiplayer only)
Players now start with 8 hearts, and gain 1x heart per 12 milestone(s).
- Rebalanced most weapons.
Most weapons do more damage, short weapons are faster, longer weapons have more reach and a longer swing.
- Made nametags on villagers and players invisible unless close.
- Villagers now give silver coins as tax payment instead of emeralds.
- Raised the chance to have twins by 10%.
- Infected villagers now have 8 hours of time before they turn.
- Changed the Amulet of Undying's texture to a transparent emerald crux.
- Added some textures to mods.
- Added an alien-like wandering trader.
- Removed some non-medieval items.
- Updated Default Controls screen.
- Added more tips.
- Minor UI changes.

## Mod Additions

### Visual & Aesthetic

**Coastal Waves** – Adds breaking waves to beaches
**Auroras** - Adds beautiful auroras to the sky
**Rainbows** - You guessed it. Rainbows!
**Wakes Reforged** – Adds water wakes for entities
**Lucent** – Dynamic lighting
**Better F3** – Enhanced debug screen layout

### Gameplay & Content

**Nirvana** – Cannabis farming and functionality
**Backpacked: World of Color** – New backpack color options
**Naturalist** – Adds realistic animals
**Riding Partners** - Ride a horse with a friend
**Blood Bits** – Pixel gore combat feedback
**Amendments** – Enhancements to vanilla block functionality
**RSEC** – Rain now extinguishes exposed campfires
**Loot Journal** (replaces Pickup Notifier) – Displays recently picked-up items
**Particular** (replaces Effective) – Adds splash, rain, and other ambient particles
**Biomancy** – Flesh-based magic and biological machines
**Chalk** – Allows players to mark navigation paths
**Quark Delight** – Adds compatibility between Farmer’s Delight and Quark
**Joy of Painting** – Create custom paintings from canvas to wall
**ImmersiveUI** – Adds animated and smooth UI transitions
**e4mc** – Enables singleplayer world sharing for co-op
**Fantasy Armor Medieval Series** – Adds detailed, decorative netherite-tier armor sets
**Ribbits** – Introduces frog-like villagers with unique trades
**Dynamic Trees – Alex’s Mobs Compat** – Adds banana plants and acacia blossom drops
**Rustic Delight** – Adds pancakes, coffee, bell peppers, and cotton
**Haunted Harvest** (replaces Corn Delight) – Adds pumpkin carving, corn, and seasonal events
**Does It Tick?** – Disables entity ticking when not visible, improving performance
