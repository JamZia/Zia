# 3.0 Changelog:
## Additions
- Returned/Fixed Singleplayer Functionality!
Play singleplayer and co-op with friends.
- Introducing a new "Reputation" system for dedicated multiplayer.
- Added (and fixed) lots of Tillage-style textures.
Carts, weapons, mobs, ui, furniture, food, BOP block textures and more added.
- Added Naturalist.
Naturalist adds more real life animals. Compatible with Alex's Mobs and a few more.
- Added Blood Bits.
Fighting now has a little bit of pixel gore.
- Added Rain should extinguish campfires.
Rain extinguish campfires if outside.
- Added Loot Journal. (Replaced Pickup Notifier)
Shows the player what they recently picked up.
- Added Particular. (Replaced Effective)
Adds particles from splashes, rain and more.
- Added Biomancy.
Flesh-related 'magic' stuff and bio-machines.
- Added Chalk.
Chalk allows you to draw marks for easier navigation in the world.
- Added Quark Delight.
An add-on for Farmer's Delight that adds compatibility with Quark.
- Added e4mc.
Adds the ability to share your singleplayer worlds. (Co-op)
- Added Fantasy Armor Medieval Series.
Adds many new netherite-tier detailed and decorational armor sets.
- Added Ribbits.
Meet and trade with Ribbits, little frog-like villagers.
- Added Does It Tick?
Performance mod. Disables entities ticking while not visible.
- Added Dynamic Trees - Alex's Mobs Compat.
Adds banana plants and acacia blossoms drops for acacia trees.
- Added Rustic Delight.
Pancakes, coffee, bell peppers and cotton.
- Added Better F3.
Improves the F3 debug screen.
- Added Starlight.
Rewrites the light engine to fix lighting performance and lighting errors.
